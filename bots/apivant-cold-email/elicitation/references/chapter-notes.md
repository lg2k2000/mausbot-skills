## Ch 13 suggested techniques by preference (book's lists)

We read preferences from behavior only and never write a type label about a customer.

- **Extroverts:** presumptive, naïveté, hanging chad, feigned disbelief, curiosity. Use nudges (nods, "Uh-huh," "I see," "Go on"); have several topics ready.
- **Introverts:** quid pro quo, word echo, curiosity, quotation of reported facts, status manipulation, bracketing. "don't finish an introvert's sentences!"; ask, then count to three.
- **Sensors:** presumptive, bracketing, quotation of reported facts, naïveté, status manipulation. Go in linear order, with facts and real examples, one topic at a time.
- **Intuitives:** presumptive, feigned or real disbelief, curiosity, empathetic exclamations, be a skeptic. Big picture and future possibilities; light on detail.
- **Thinkers:** bracketing, exploiting the instinct to complain, feigned or real disbelief, quotation of reported facts, curiosity. Cause and effect, consequences, fairness.
- **Feelers:** third-party perspective, empathetic exclamations, presumptive, naïveté, be a skeptic. Points of agreement, appreciation, legitimacy of feelings.
- **Judgers:** presumptive, quid pro quo, quotation of reported facts, word echo, feigned disbelief. Be organized; send relevant information ahead.
- **Perceivers:** feigned or real disbelief, word echo, curiosity, status manipulation, bracketing. Give choices; don't force quick decisions.

In this skill, feigned disbelief, demotion and invented reported facts stay out whatever the type.
