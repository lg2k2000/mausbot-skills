# Chapter Notes: The JOLT Effect (Matthew Dixon and Ted McKenna, 2022)

From a cover-to-cover read of the 2022 epub: preface, intro, Ch 1-11, acknowledgments, notes. Figures were viewed as images. The index was not read.

| Ch | Title | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intro | Stuck | Why deals stall even when the buyer wants to change. |
| 1 | The Inaction Paradox | Status quo vs. indecision. Omission bias (errors of commission vs. omission). The three drivers of indecision: valuation problems, lack of information, outcome uncertainty. The "silent killer" of sales. |
| 2 | The JOLT Effect | The four moves: Judge the indecision, Offer your recommendation, Limit the exploration, Take risk off the table. |
| 3 | Judge the Indecision | Pinpoint the source. Gauge personal indecisiveness: how they search for and consume information, how they evaluate alternatives, content with "good enough" or not, nature of delay tactics. Exacerbating factors (importance, time pressure). Scorecard (Fig 3.2: four rows scored 1-7, total 4-28; more than 22 points = disqualify or deprioritize). Powerful requests; ask directly. |
| 4 | Offer Your Recommendation | The choice dilemma (the jelly-tasting study). Two skills: proactive guidance and personal advocacy. How not to amplify indecision; recommend before confusion is voiced. |
| 5 | Limit the Exploration | Own the flow of information. Anticipate needs and objections. Radical candor. Two different soundtracks: winners talk 58% vs. 52% in losses; cooperative overlapping; purposeful silence has diminishing returns. |
| 6 | Take Risk Off the Table | Set outcome expectations, offer downside risk protection, start smaller. |
| 7 | Becoming a "Buyer's Agent" | The principal-agent problem. Overcoming the agency dilemma: don't let them overbuy, credit competitors, admit gaps. When "yes" is the easiest choice. |
| 8 | Beyond Win Rates | Four flavors of loyalty. Post-decision dysfunction (worrying, checking, decision instability). How JOLT lowers customer effort. |
| 9 | How Much Is Indecision Costing You? | Acceptable no-decision rate, "age out" dates, interaction velocity, coaching the middle 60%. Assessing skill: manual call audits (small samples, binary scoring, too many goals), structured customer feedback (skews to wins), conversation intelligence (insight, action and cost pitfalls). |
| 10 | Applying JOLT in Different Sales Environments | Inbound vs. outbound. Long-cycle sales: demos hide objections, excessive probing, de-risking when an opt-out isn't an option. |
| 11 | Building the JOLT-Capable Sales Force | Hiring and developing JOLT sellers; JOLT as an overlay; persuasion to motivation; managers versus "happy ears". |
