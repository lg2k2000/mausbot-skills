# Three-whys deck

Adapted from the three-whys skill in the marketplace EBR & Value Deck Builder (Krista Letz). Liam picked it Nov 12 2026 11:22 PM MT ("do the three whys deck setup"). All decks go through Mac Operator with Claude Code (Liam, Nov 5 2026). HPE collateral is cleared generic public material with no HPE data-policy gate (Liam, Nov 8 2026); don't add one.

Owners:

- Copy and facts: the requesting CRO. HPE CRO for HPE accounts, Toolbox CRO for Toolbox prospects and clients.
- Approval: Liam, on the exact copy, in the requester's chat.
- The file: Mac Operator.
- HPE Enablement may answer product facts when HPE CRO asks. It never makes collateral.
- General Toolbox pitch decks (not tied to one account) come from Toolbox CRO too (Liam, Nov 13 2026): the same copy step and build step, Toolbox brand kit, without the 5-slide shape unless it fits.

## The deck: 5 slides

1. Why change: the problem in the account's own public words or from our call (quote, speaker role, date, source), and what it costs them only if a number is sourced.
2. Why now: the dated trigger (renewal, licensing change, refresh cycle, leadership statement, deadline). No trigger, no deck: tell Liam instead.
3. Why us: two or three proof points from approved proof only (the VME Knowledge page for HPE; Toolbox results Liam has approved). No invented customers or results.
4. Next step: the one next yes, with who and when if it was agreed.
5. Sources: every number, quote and claim mapped to its source and date. If Liam wants four slides, this moves to speaker notes.

Rules:

- Every number traces to a source, a call (full transcript read), or Liam. No source, no number.
- Lead with outcomes. No ARR, spend or per-user dollars unless Liam asks by name. Private pricing notes never appear.
- HPE: public sources plus HPE CRO's own deal and account pages (an account brief from deal-anatomy-brief is the best input). No HPE mailbox content.

## Copy step (requesting CRO)

1. Draft the slide text with writing-mega-skill and run its final gate yourself. Decks skip Copy Humanizer.
2. Check every line against its source.
3. Show Liam the copy and the source list in your chat and get his yes on that exact copy.
4. Send Mac Operator one brief (SendToAgent f8fcaa88-214e-4506-b47c-098dd0d20c68): account, goal, the approved copy word for word, the source list, format (16:9 .pptx plus a PDF unless Liam says otherwise), brand, and where the files go.

## Build step (Mac Operator)

- Run it as a normal Mac job with Claude Code. Use the approved copy exactly. Send copy or fact problems back to the requester; never reword.
- Brand: Toolbox decks use the Toolbox brand kit. HPE decks are generic, not HPE-branded, unless Liam gives a template. When Liam gives a sample deck or template, save its palette, fonts and logo rules as a brand kit in that business's folder and reuse it.
- Compatibility: open the .pptx in a second app on the Mac (Keynote, or LibreOffice if it's installed) and export the PDF from there, so it opens cleanly in PowerPoint and Google Slides. Installing LibreOffice needs Liam's yes.
- QA: render slide images or a contact sheet and look at every slide (text fits, nothing overflows, numbers match the sources slide). Compare with any earlier version.
- Deliver per your profile: the file to Liam in your chat, then one note to the requester.
- Never send the deck to the customer.
